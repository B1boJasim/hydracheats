# HYDRA CHEATS --- FAQ

## Is HydraCheats 100% safe or undetectable?

No. The official HydraCheats FAQ itself states that no cheat is 100%
safe and that there is always some risk. Anti-cheat systems and
detection methods can change.

## How long are licenses valid?

All currently advertised licenses are lifetime licenses. HydraCheats
states that future updates are included with no subscription or hidden
recurring fee.

## Which Windows versions are supported?

HydraCheats currently advertises support for Windows 10 and Windows 11,
both 64-bit. Older Windows versions are not officially supported.

The official FAQ currently lists:

-   PayPal

## Is there a free trial?

No. HydraCheats currently states that it does not offer a free trial.

## Are refunds available?

No. The current official FAQ states that refunds are not offered.

## How do I purchase HydraCheats?

The current official FAQ directs customers to contact HydraCheats
through Discord.

## How quickly is the key delivered?

The website advertises delivery within approximately 15 minutes via
email or Discord.

## Is HydraCheats Stream Proof?

HydraCheats currently advertises Stream Proof for OBS Studio and
Streamlabs.

## Does it work on every FiveM server?

The product is designed for FiveM, but server configurations and
anti-cheat systems differ. Universal compatibility cannot be guaranteed.
