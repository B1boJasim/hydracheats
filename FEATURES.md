HydraCheats products. Feature availability can change between releases.

-   EasyAdmin Unban

## Stream Proof

HydraCheats currently advertises Stream Proof for:

-   OBS Studio
-   Streamlabs

The official FAQ specifically limits the current Stream Proof claim to
OBS Studio and Streamlabs.

## Updates

HydraCheats advertises regular updates. Lifetime licenses are advertised
as including future updates without a recurring subscription.
